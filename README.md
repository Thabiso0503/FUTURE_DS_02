# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer retention and churn behavior using customer subscription data. The objective is to identify why customers leave, understand retention trends, and generate actionable business insights that can help improve customer engagement and long-term business growth.

Customer retention is a key performance area for SaaS, subscription-based, fintech, and digital businesses because reducing churn helps improve profitability and customer lifetime value.

This analysis was completed as part of the **Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Task 2 (2026)** internship program.

---

## Business Problem
Businesses often struggle with customer churn, where users stop using a service or cancel subscriptions. High churn negatively affects revenue, growth, and customer lifetime value.

This project aims to answer important business questions such as:

- Why are customers leaving the platform?
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
- How long do customers remain active?
- What factors influence customer retention?
- What strategies can improve retention and reduce churn?

---

## Project Objectives
The main objectives of this analysis were to:

1. Clean and prepare customer subscription data
2. Analyze customer churn and retention trends
3. Identify high-risk customer segments
4. Explore customer lifetime patterns
5. Perform customer cohort analysis
6. Generate business recommendations to improve retention
7. Build a professional dashboard/report for stakeholders

## Analysis Performed
The following business analyses were conducted:

### 1. Churn Analysis
Identifying customers who stopped using the platform and understanding churn patterns.

### 2. Retention Trends
Analyzing how customer retention changes over time.

### 3. Customer Segmentation
Identifying customer groups with higher churn probability based on subscription type, region, or activity level.

### 4. Cohort Analysis
Grouping customers by signup period to analyze long-term retention behavior.

### 5. Customer Lifetime Patterns
Understanding how long customers typically remain active before churning.
